Daily SeaVector Protocol

Execute this in order. Do not improvise until the checklist is complete.

1. Morning Scan — 30 minCheck YachtWorld, broker sites/social, marina updates, transporters, Google Maps, NOAA/NHC. Look for sold posts, price cuts, relocation clues, slip pressure, storm triggers, and transport capacity.
2. Log Operators FirstIf a broker, marina, transporter, captain, or yard matters, create or update the operator record before logging calls. One master operator record only.
3. Call Block — 3 to 5 touchesCall/message operators. Ask operational questions: movement direction, transport timing, marina pressure, route demand, referral policy, and who else you should know.
4. Log Every Touch ImmediatelyUse + Call. Select the existing operator or quick-create a new one. Record exact useful phrases, names mentioned, follow-up actions, and tone.
5. Create Opportunities Only When RealUse + Opportunity only for actual coordination angles: transport need, empty route, marina fit, referral opening, distressed vessel, or follow-up with a clear next action.
6. Evening Review — 15 minReview next actions, update operator intelligence fields, raise/lower relationship scores, identify one pattern, and export backup if you added important data.

Operating Rule

Operator records are now intelligence profiles. Track value, trust, reach, movement patterns, triggers, and preferred communication so SeaVector can identify who actually matters operationally.
